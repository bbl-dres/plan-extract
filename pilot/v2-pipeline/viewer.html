Using the viewer

DragPan (2D) or rotate (3D; right-drag or Shift-drag pans)
WheelZoom at the cursor
HoverOutlines a room, opening, void or the walls
ClickPins it: the popup stays until × or Esc, a click elsewhere replaces it
1–9, 0Toggle the first ten layers in list order
← → or [ ]Previous and next plan of the filtered list
H / Shift+HLater and earlier version of the plan (v1, v2, v3, benchmark models); the History block under Details compares them
FFit to rooms

Everything shown is read from viewer-data/ (written by export_viewer.py; #data=local reads the local export under data/out/); nothing is uploaded. Review checks, the panel state and the sheet filter are kept in this browser only. Upload, the confirmation steps before a run, editing and the downloads of the workflow study need the app and are not part of this viewer.
