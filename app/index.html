How this works

  1. Upload: one sheet per floor plan (PDF, JPG, PNG, TIFF, DXF), or the demo sheet. Drawing, title block and scale are read.
  2. Building area: confirm the detected outline. The whole building inside, title block and legend outside.
  3. Scale: check the scale that was read. If it is missing or wrong, measure a known distance.
  4. Results: walls, openings and rooms appear as they are found (about a minute). Review the rooms, then download JSON, Excel, DXF, IFC or CSV.

Everything runs locally in app/data/ (gitignored). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
