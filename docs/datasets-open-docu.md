# Public Building Documentation as a Data Source

*Research survey · 7 October 2026 · Building documentation published by public building owners in Switzerland, Germany, Austria and abroad – Bautendokumentationen, project leaflets, competition reports and campus area plans – as evaluation data with published area figures (a noisy reference, not ground truth: §2, finding 11) and as drawing-style references for BBL. Companion catalogues: [open datasets](datasets-open.md) and [closed and semi-public datasets](datasets-closed.md). Bracketed numbers refer to the shared [source list](papers/sources.md).*

## 1. Scope and Method

**Question.** Which public building owners publish documentation with floor plans and official area figures? In what form, in what numbers and under which terms?

**Corpus.** About 75 publishers:
- Start points: BBL's Bautendokumentationen page\[536\] and the kennwerte-db repository\[537\]. Its source inventory lists 13 Swiss publishers\[538\]. Its database holds 373 documents from six of them (BBL, armasuisse, Stadt Zürich, Stadt Bern, Stadt St. Gallen, Kanton Aargau), with GF extracted for 115\[539\].
- Switzerland: 20 cantons and cities in all language regions, the ETH domain, SBB, Swiss Post and two competition platforms.
- Germany: the federal building administration, 15 state building administrations and BKI.
- Austria: BIG, ARE, Burghauptmannschaft and the Länder.
- International: ten national owners in the Nordics, Benelux, France, Ireland and Luxembourg; GSA and the Library of Congress (USA); UK, Canadian and Australian bodies; three universities that publish area plans.

**Method.**
- Listing pages were read and items counted on 7 October 2026. "Counted" means links on the listing page; estimates say how they were made.
- Terms of use are quoted from each site's legal, imprint or copyright page.
- 20 sample PDFs and one HABS sheet were opened, at most one or two per publisher (§7). For each we checked vector paths per page, embedded rasters and their size, scale notes, labels and the area table. Thousands of vector paths on a plan page mean vector drawings.
- No bulk downloads and no registration. Several archives block automated access; their terms come from search-engine extracts and are marked as such.

**Conventions.**
- Terms verdicts follow the [open datasets catalogue](datasets-open.md#1-scope-and-method): P, SA, ND, NC, R, U. This survey adds two:
  - **C** = copyright reserved, no reuse licence. Viewing, downloading and printing for own use only; any reproduction, public or commercial use needs written consent.
  - **PD** = public domain.
- Relevance:
  - High = many items with plans and official areas for office, administrative or historical buildings, or a large licence-clean style source.
  - Medium = useful, but few items, plans or areas unverified, areas without plans, or restrictive terms.
  - Low = no plans, no areas, very few items or the wrong building type.
- Uses:
  - Evaluation = internal measurement against published areas; documents are not redistributed and never enter production weights.
  - Style reference = drawing conventions for the renderer and for test-set design.
  - Training = only after written rights clearance.
- Area terms:
  - SIA 416: GF (gross floor area), NGF (net floor area), HNF (main usable area), GV (building volume). French SP, SUP and VB; Italian SP and VE.
  - DIN 277: BGF, NRF or NGF, NUF, BRI.
  - Austria: ÖNORM B 1800. Norway: NS 3940 (BTA). USA: gross and assignable square feet.

## 2. Key Findings

1. **Swiss owners publish far more than kennwerte-db covers.**
   - About 1,400 documentations of completed projects from 19 Swiss publishers are online, plus about 550 jury reports. kennwerte-db holds 373 from six.
   - Large series it does not cover: Kanton Bern AGG (226 PDFs, 1975–2024)\[540\], Kanton Zürich (290 completed projects, an estimated 460 PDFs)\[541\], Canton de Vaud (384 PDFs, about 146 project plaquettes)\[542\], Winterthur (102)\[543\] and Solothurn (92)\[544\].
   - Earlier counts were off. Stadt St. Gallen numbers 217 documentations but has 78 online\[545\]. Basel-Stadt has 62 online, not about 180\[546\]. French- and Italian-speaking owners do publish series (Vaud, Valais, Ticino, Lausanne, Ville de Genève).
2. **Areas are building totals; plans show selected floors.**
   - Nearly every document gives SIA 416 GF, HNF and GV, or DIN 277 BGF, NGF and BRI, for the whole building, not per floor.
   - Most show two to four representative floors: BBL, Stadt Zürich, SIB Sachsen, Lausanne, Winterthur.
   - Complete floor sets were found in four samples: Kanton Zürich's Einweihungsdokumentation (every floor at M 1:250 with room names)\[547\], armasuisse Kriens (all four floors)\[548\], Vaud No. 114\[549\] and Ticino's Stabile Amministrativo Lugano\[550\]. Only such documents support a sum-of-floors check against GF without assumptions.
3. **Recent documents have vector plans; archives are scans.**
   - Every InDesign PDF sampled from 2011 onwards carries vector plans: BBL 2011 and 2023 (7,000–113,000 paths per page)\[551\]\[552\], Stadt Zürich 2024 (89,000)\[553\], armasuisse 2017, Kanton Zürich 2013, Ticino and SIB Sachsen 2024\[554\].
   - Kanton Bern's archive is 300-dpi scans with an OCR layer (1992 sample)\[555\].
   - Web versions are often downsampled rasters: Lausanne about 150 dpi, Winterthur 992 × 1,403 px pages\[556\], Solothurn 863 × 600 px plans\[557\].
   - Vector plans give exact reference geometry: walls and rooms can be read from the PDF and the page rasterised at any resolution.
4. **Scale is stated in about half the series.**
   - Scale notes or bars appear in Stadt Zürich ("Massstab 1:750" and a bar), Kanton Zürich (M 1:250), Stadt Bern ("1:200 / A3"), armasuisse ("M 1:500"), Winterthur ("1 : 500"), Ticino and St. Gallen (bars), and Solothurn (1:150).
   - BBL floor plans, Vaud and SIB carry none. Scale must then be calibrated, for example against the published GF – a test of the pipeline's scale step.
5. **Room stamps are almost absent.**
   - Brochure plans show walls, doors, stairs and furniture. Rooms are unlabelled (BBL, Stadt Zürich, SIB, Vaud), named (Kanton Zürich, St. Gallen) or keyed by number (Kanton Bern, Winterthur).
   - Stamps with room number and area appear only in three sources: Solothurn's project reports (facility-management plans, illegible at web resolution), the Dutch Binnenhof design documents (raster, redacted)\[558\] and Portland State University's area plans (USA)\[559\].
   - Brochures therefore test walls, rooms, scale and GF, not room-stamp OCR.
6. **No source licenses its plans for reuse.**
   - Every Swiss federal, cantonal and city site reserves its rights. admin.ch requires "die schriftliche Zustimmung der Urheberrechtsträger" for any reproduction\[560\].
   - The plans are works of the credited architects.
   - Closest to permission:
     - Aargau, Solothurn and Baden exempt "ausdrücklich zur Weiterverwendung publizierten Materialien wie Broschüren"\[561\]\[562\].
     - The Burghauptmannschaft (Austria) allows non-commercial reprint with attribution\[563\].
     - Bygningsstyrelsen (Denmark) applies CC BY-NC-ND to its reports\[564\].
     - Basel-Stadt's CC BY 4.0 and the Dutch CC0 cover only a project list and texts\[565\]\[566\].
   - Some publishers explicitly forbid processing: BLB NRW bans "Einspeicherung oder Verarbeitung in Datensysteme"\[567\], Schwyz bans storing content in databases\[568\], and BKI reserves text and data mining under § 44b(3) UrhG\[569\].
7. **BBL's and armasuisse's own documentations are the easiest to use.**
   - BBL publishes 148 documentations and armasuisse 53, all of federal buildings whose CAD plans and room data sit in federal systems\[536\]\[570\].
   - Pairing the brochure plan (input, in publication style) with BBL's own CAD geometry (reference) gives per-floor and per-room evaluation without the per-building limit. The published area figures are not the reference (finding 11).
   - The rights question becomes an internal one between the federal client and its planners.
8. **Germany has one comparable series.**
   - SIB Sachsen publishes 126 project leaflets (2010–2026) with vector plans and BGF, NGF and BRI, including tax offices, courts and historical buildings\[571\].
   - BBR, Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg and Berlin publish project pages with rounded areas and no plans.
   - BKI holds the richest DIN 277 data (3,903 objects in its free index, 123 per Objektdaten volume), but it is commercial and reserves text and data mining\[572\]\[573\].
9. **Austria and the rest of Europe add style references, not ground truth.**
   - BIG has 90 project brochures, but its 2019 sample has no plans\[574\].
   - The Burghauptmannschaft has 56 Baufolder on historic federal buildings (not opened)\[575\]; Senaatti has about 55 building-history surveys of heritage government buildings\[576\].
   - Statsbygg, Bygningsstyrelsen, SFV, Akademiska Hus, Regie der Gebouwen and OPPIC give at most one area figure per project and no plans.
10. **HABS is the only large public-domain plan source.**
    - The Library of Congress holds more than 581,000 HABS/HAER/HALS drawings, photographs and histories for over 43,000 structures, "considered to be in the public domain"\[577\]\[578\]. An estimated 1,000–2,000 of the structures are courthouses, city halls, post offices and offices.
    - Sheets are 400-dpi bitonal scans with scale bars and room labels, but no areas\[579\]. They are a training and style source for hand-drafted historical plans.
    - GSA's Design Excellence and BIM pages now return HTTP 404. What remains on gsa.gov is area data without plans\[580\].
11. **Published area figures are a noisy reference, not ground truth.**
    - BBL's own experience: figures in Bautendokumentationen are often entered by hand and are not always derived from the geometry of the published plans. The same caution applies to every publisher's key figures and to any manually maintained area record.
    - Use them for plausibility checks with tolerances, and measure how far they deviate from the geometry; that deviation is a data-quality finding in its own right.
    - Ground truth comes from geometry: CAD polygons that pass plan-check, or polygons annotated on the plan itself.

## 5. Rights and Terms

Not legal advice; open questions belong with BBL's legal service.

- **Swiss federal sites.** admin.ch: "Durch das Herunterladen oder Kopieren von Inhalten, Bildern, Fotos oder anderen Dateien werden keinerlei Rechte bezüglich der Inhalte übertragen. … Für die Reproduktion jeglicher Elemente ist die schriftliche Zustimmung der Urheberrechtsträger im Voraus einzuholen."\[560\] No open-data licence covers the Bautendokumentationen.
- **Cantons and cities.** The same pattern: view, download and print for own use; anything further needs written consent. Exceptions and variants:
  - Aargau, Solothurn and Baden exempt brochures "ausdrücklich zur Weiterverwendung publiziert". It is unclear whether this covers architects' plans or model training.
  - Ticino may allow non-commercial use in writing on request.
  - Schwyz and BLB NRW explicitly forbid storing or processing content in databases.
- **Architects' copyright.** Plans are protected works: the Swiss Copyright Act lists "Pläne" among technical works and "Werke der Baukunst" separately (Art. 2 para. 2 lit. d and e URG)\[400\]. The brochures credit architects, photographers and authors. A publisher's terms cannot grant more than the publisher holds. The exemption for official works (Art. 5 URG: laws, decisions, minutes and reports of authorities) probably does not extend to promotional brochures, and not to the third-party plans they reproduce – to be confirmed by the legal service.
- **Internal evaluation versus training.**
  - Evaluation: copies stay inside the administration, are not redistributed, and results are reported as metrics. Two exceptions may apply; whether either covers BBL's use is for the legal service:
    - Art. 19 para. 1 lit. c URG allows copies "in Betrieben, öffentlichen Verwaltungen … für die interne Information oder Dokumentation".
    - Art. 24d URG allows reproduction for scientific research where a technical process requires it and access is lawful.
  - Training: model weights derived from the plans are a further use. It needs written consent from the publisher and, for third-party plans, from the architects. BBL's own documentations are the exception: there, BBL's planner contracts may already grant usage rights.
- **Abroad.**
  - Germany allows text and data mining unless the rights holder reserves it in machine-readable form (§ 44b UrhG)\[529\]; BKI has reserved it.
  - HABS is public domain.
  - The OGL (UK, Canada) and the Dutch CC0 exclude third-party material.

## 6. Gaps

1. **Per-floor area ground truth.** Almost no source gives areas per floor. The exceptions:
   - Solothurn project reports (only the floors shown)
   - Graubünden pages (by building part)
   - some armasuisse documents
   - Portland State and Coventry (Coventry unverified)
2. **Room stamps with number, usage and area.** Swiss brochures have none. The only examples are low-resolution facility-management plans (Solothurn), redacted rasters (Rijksvastgoedbedrijf) and US campus plans (Portland State).
3. **A reuse licence for plans.** None exists; the brochure carve-outs of Aargau, Solothurn and Baden are untested.
4. **Source drawings.** No publisher releases DWG or IFC files. Ticino's, BLB Brandenburg's and the Burghauptmannschaft's CAD guidelines give conventions only, and BIG's need a login.
5. **Historical plans with areas.** Kanton Bern's scans from 1975 onwards have GF; HABS has no areas; BBL documentations before 1999 are not online.
6. **A machine-readable index.** Only kennwerte-db (GF for 115 documents) and Graubünden's pages are machine-readable. kennwerte-db extracted GF for only 4 of 55 Stadt Bern flyers, although most contain it.
7. **Unverified series.** For each of these, one sample would settle plan form and areas:
   - Swiss: Valais, Ville de Genève, Basel-Stadt, Graubünden, Thurgau, Kanton St. Gallen PDFs
   - Austrian: Burghauptmannschaft Baufolder, older BIG brochures
   - Other: Coventry, BCIT

## 7. Limitations of This Survey

- Not legal advice. Terms are quoted as found on 7 October 2026.
- **Samples opened (one each unless stated).** Everything else is inferred from listing pages, page text, file names, kennwerte-db extractions or search-engine extracts.
  - Swiss: BBL (2), armasuisse, Stadt Zürich, Kanton Bern AGG, Kanton Zürich, Solothurn, Winterthur, Vaud, Ticino, Lausanne
  - Germany: SIB Sachsen, a Bavarian project sheet, BKI sample pages
  - Austria and Europe: BIG, Senaatti, Rijksvastgoedbedrijf
  - USA and UK: Portland State, the GSA 2022 awards book, the UK hub brochure, one HABS sheet
- **Counts change.** Some figures are estimates: Kanton Zürich PDFs, the HABS public-building share, Historic England and PROV holdings.
- **Blocked or unavailable sites.** Terms for blocked sites come from search extracts.
  - Blocked automated access: loc.gov, Historic England, trove.scot, PROV, Museums of History NSW, Basel-Landschaft (Cloudflare).
  - Other failures: Schaffhausen (server error), Niedersachsen (timeout), BImA (unreadable script application).
- **Not assessed.**
  - Print-only material: the 2016 Bundesbauten book, BBR's Jahrbuch, Stadt Zürich documentations before 2008, Kanton St. Gallen print copies.
  - Paywalled or login-only material: konkurado, BKI's full books, BIG's guidelines.
  - SBB listing PDFs.
- **Area definitions vary.** Areas are quoted as published, and the definitions differ:
  - SIA 416 editions 1993 and 2003
  - older volumes per SIA 116
  - HNF per SIA d 0165
  - DIN 277 terms without the edition named
- **Leads not followed.**
  - Norway and the Netherlands: Forsvarsbygg gross-area measurement reports; Dutch planning annexes with BVO calculations.
  - Liechtenstein's Hochbautenbericht.
  - Credit bills with possible plans: Luxembourg parliamentary bills, Geneva's Grand Conseil credit bills, Vaud's credit decrees.
  - Munich council attachments.
  - Room data: ETH Zurich and EPFL room-information services.
