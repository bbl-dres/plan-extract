# Commercial and Semi-Public Data Sources for Floor Plan Extraction

*Research catalogue · 7 October 2026 · Where BBL could obtain floor plans for training and evaluation beyond the [public datasets](datasets-open.md): books, digital libraries and archives, museums, competitions, commercial vendors and partners, with each source's licence or terms as stated. Tools and products are in [solutions (closed)](solutions-closed.md) and [solutions (open)](solutions-open.md), public owners' building documentation in [open documentation sources](datasets-open-docu.md), and BBL's data needs in [motivation and goals](motivation-goals.md). Bracketed numbers refer to the shared [source list](papers/sources.md).*

## 1. Scope and Method

**Corpus.** 84 entries covering about 130 named sources, in six groups (§4): books and publisher databases, digital archives and libraries, museums and collections, architecture competitions, commercial vendors and services, and other routes such as partnerships. Public ML datasets are in [datasets-open.md](datasets-open.md) and are not repeated. Public owners' own building documentation (BBL, cantonal and city building departments, international equivalents) is in [datasets-open-docu.md](datasets-open-docu.md).

**Method.** Web research on 7 October 2026. Sources:
- publisher and archive pages, terms of use, general terms and conditions (GTC/AGB) and imprints;
- robots.txt and TDMRep files;
- single count queries against public APIs: Europeana, Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek (DDB), Met, Art Institute of Chicago, Rijksmuseum, Smithsonian, Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, the Swiss Federal Archives, OpenStreetMap Overpass, and the OAI-PMH and IIIF endpoints of e-periodica, e-rara and e-manuscripta.

Nothing was downloaded, registered for or bought. Counts are as of 7 October 2026 for the query stated; keyword counts are noisy and include elevations, sections and maps.

**Conventions.**
- Tables use Source (linked) | Type | Content and scope | Format and access | Cost | Licence or terms | Suitability | Effort | Relevance.
- Licence verdicts reuse the letters of [datasets-open.md](datasets-open.md#1-scope-and-method): P = permissive (public domain, CC0, CC BY); SA = share-alike; ND = no derivatives; NC = non-commercial; R = research or academic use only; U = unclear or conflicting. Two letters are added here:
  - C = all rights reserved or contractual terms; use needs a licence or written permission, which can be negotiated;
  - X = terms or a machine-readable reservation expressly exclude scraping, text and data mining (TDM) or AI training; a licence may still be negotiable.
- Training means data that may end up in production weights. Evaluation means internal benchmarking only, never in production weights.
- Relevance: High = Swiss, office, administrative or historical public-building plans in varied styles, with a realistic licence route. Medium = useful for evaluation or style diversity, or large but licence-bound. Low = wrong domain, closed terms, or impractical.

**Quality of the evidence.**
- The shared web-search allowance of the research session ran out partway through; later checks used direct page reads and APIs only.
- Several sites blocked automated reading (HTTP 403, JavaScript-only pages, bot checks): fedlex (read via file store), loc.gov, RIBApix, Gallica, Het Nieuwe Instituut, Comparis, newhome, CubiCasa, Zillow, swiss-architects.com. Park Books and Scheidegger & Spiess were not read because their robots.txt disallows all agents.
- Quotes marked "(snippet)" come from search-result extracts, not the page itself.
- Plan counts marked "estimate" are orders of magnitude, with their basis stated.

## 2. Key Findings

1. **Nothing is licence-clean at scale for BBL's core case.**
   - No vendor sells annotated office or administrative plans. The only priced offer is custom annotation: LXT quotes "$10K for … 1,000-5,000 floor plans" up to "$80K+".\[370\]
   - Every plan-rich Swiss source is rights-reserved, and systematic storage or reuse needs written consent: e-periodica, KdS-online, Quart, Hochparterre, gta Archiv and Staatsarchiv Zürich.
2. **The best in-domain data sits with peer public owners.**
   - CADexchange's owner members include BBL, armasuisse Immobilien, Kanton Zürich (Hochbauamt and Immobilienamt), Basel-Stadt, Stadt Zürich (Amt für Hochbauten), Graubünden, Zug, Solothurn, Basel-Landschaft and the Kantonsspital St. Gallen.\[349\]
   - Their CAD guidelines require the same elements as BBL's CAD-Richtlinie: one closed room polygon per room, a floor polygon per storey, and room stamps. In Basel-Stadt these are layers `A1ZD22_RAUMPOLYGON` and `A1ZD21_GESCHOSSPOLYGON`; Immobilien Stadt Bern defines the same polygons, room stamps and room numbers for its CAFM.\[371\]\[372\]
   - A data pool through CADexchange or KBOB is the only route found to labelled office, school and administrative plans at scale.
3. **Openly licensed historical plans exist in volume, but outside Switzerland.**
   - Architekturmuseum TU Berlin: about 91,000 CC0 images on Europeana, 20,685 openly licensed "Grundriss" images, and 7,070 DDB hits for administrative-building terms, mostly Prussian and German public buildings of the 19th and early 20th centuries.\[373\]\[374\]\[375\]
   - HABS/HAER/HALS: 72,828 measured-drawing sheets, US government work with "No known restrictions".\[376\]\[377\]
   - Both add drawing styles under clean licences, not Swiss office buildings or room stamps. TU München's comparable 24,729 "Grundriss" images are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.\[375\]
4. **e-periodica is the largest digitised body of Swiss building plans.**
   - Holdings: Schweizerische Bauzeitung 1883–1978, Das Werk 1914–1976, Werk, Bauen + Wohnen 1980–2024, Bauen + Wohnen 1947–1979 and Hochparterre 1988–2025. That is roughly 300,000 pages and an estimated 10,000–30,000 printed plans, with IIIF, OAI-PMH and a DOI per article.\[378\]\[379\]
   - The terms limit use to "Einzelpersonen für die Nutzung zu privaten und nicht kommerziellen Zwecken sowie zu Bildungszwecken". Systematic storage needs the written consent of ETH Library and the rights holders.\[380\]
5. **Federal archives hold plans of BBL's own buildings and of its predecessors' buildings.**
   - The Swiss Federal Archives keep plans of "civilian and military federal real estate" in the real-estate contracts and plans fonds (K3\*, 1848–2002). It is searchable only in the reading room, and digitisation on order is free.\[381\]\[382\]
   - The National Library's Federal Archive for Monument Preservation (EAD) holds about 110,000 plans of historic buildings, including the SIA Bürgerhaus archive.\[383\]
   - Both need an agreement between federal offices. The architects' copyright remains.
6. **Building Types Online is the only licensable digital plan database found at a publisher.**
   - De Gruyter's database has about 8,000 drawings, "a large part" vector-based, including 137 office and 210 educational buildings, at €299 per year for individuals.\[384\]\[385\]
   - De Gruyter's terms reserve commercial TDM to De Gruyter and forbid systematic download, so training needs a negotiated licence.\[386\]
   - The other plan-rich books are print only (Hochparterre, Quart, DOM, Neufert) or view only for non-members (GSK's KdS-online).
7. **Machine-readable reservations are now common in the EU.**
   - De Gruyter's robots.txt reserves commercial TDM under §44b UrhG; Springer Nature's TDMRep file sets `tdm-reservation: 1`; ImmoScout24.de declares a §44b(3) reservation in its imprint.\[387\]\[388\]\[389\]
   - Bloomsbury, JSTOR and Matterport prohibit AI training in their terms. Matterport also prohibits validating and benchmarking.\[390\]\[391\]\[392\]
8. **Competition material carries no training rights.**
   - Copyright of entries stays with the participants (SIA 142 Art. 26.1, KBOB Doc 21). SIA 142i-101d limits entrants' digital data to documenting the competition.\[393\]\[394\]
   - simap tender documents reach only registered bidders and carry each owner's reuse conditions.\[395\]
   - konkurado (about 3,400 competitions since 1937) and Hochparterre Wettbewerbe (more than 15,000 plans and images) are evaluation material at most, after consent.\[396\]\[397\]
9. **Portals and project databases are closed.**
   - SMG's GTC for Homegate and ImmoScout24 forbid systematic selection "(e.g. by scraping)" and linking content "with other data".\[398\]
   - ArchDaily forbids any exploitation without prior written consent.\[399\]
   - Listing plans belong to the advertisers and are mostly residential.
10. **Swiss law is more permissive than many terms suggest, but unsettled.**
    - Art. 24d URG allows remuneration-free copies for scientific research, commercial research included, with lawful access and without an opt-out.\[400\]\[401\]
    - Whether it covers AI training is for the courts to decide, according to the Swiss Federal Institute of Intellectual Property (IPI). The IPI plans a consultation draft by the end of 2026.\[402\]
    - Internal evaluation fits the exception better than training production models (§5).

Rules that apply to all competition material:
- **Copyright stays with the participants.** SIA 142 Art. 26.1 (quoted in SIA 142i-101d): "Bei allen Wettbewerben (Studienaufträgen) verbleibt das Urheberrecht an den Wettbewerbsbeiträgen (Studien) bei den Teilnehmern". The owner receives only the physical documents of prize-winning entries.\[393\]
- **Entrants' digital data serve documentation only.** SIA 142i-101d §17.2: "Sie dienen ausschliesslich zur Dokumentation des Wettbewerbs nach der Beurteilung. Eine anderweitige Verwendung der digitalen Daten ist nicht gestattet."\[393\]
- **The 2025 revision of SIA 142/143** leaves rights of use to agreement (secondary source).\[490\]
- **KBOB recommends settling use rights in the tender documents.** Its guide: "Es empfiehlt sich, den Umgang mit Immaterialgüterrechten sowie allfälligen Nutzungsrechten … in den Ausschreibungsunterlagen detailliert zu regeln".\[491\] KBOB Doc 21 repeats: "Das Urheberrecht an den Wettbewerbsbeiträgen verbleibt bei den Teilnehmenden".\[394\]

## 5. Legal Notes

**Not legal advice.** BBL's legal service must assess every source before use, benchmarking included.

**Copyright in plans.**
- **Plans are protected works.** The URG lists "Werke mit wissenschaftlichem oder technischem Inhalt wie Zeichnungen, Pläne, Karten" and "Werke der Baukunst" (Art. 2 para. 2 lit. d, e).\[400\]
- **The threshold is low.** For architects the URG "lässt einen geringen Grad selbständiger Tätigkeit genügen" but denies protection to "bloss eine handwerkliche Leistung" (BGE 117 II 466).\[522\] Plans of a protected work are protected "indépendamment du fait que la construction ait été réalisée ou non" (BGE 125 III 328).\[523\] Routine plans may fall below the threshold; this is decided case by case.
- **Rights stay with the architect.** The author is the natural person who created the work (Art. 6). Owning the sheets or the building gives no usage rights (Art. 16 para. 3). Protection lasts 70 years after the author's death, counted from 31 December (Art. 29, 32), so plans by authors who died in 1955 or earlier are free from 2026.\[400\]
- **Scans create no new right.** Art. 2 para. 3bis protects photographs of three-dimensional objects only. The dispatch: "Die Fotokopie oder andere Formen der Wiedergabe von … Plänen … sind folglich nicht schutzfähig".\[401\] Archive and library terms still bind as contracts.
- **Plans in authority files remain protected.** Art. 5 exempts "Entscheidungen, Protokolle und Berichte von Behörden"; plans that architects submit, for example in permit files, are arguably not such reports.\[400\]
- **BBL's rights in plans of its own buildings come from contracts.** KBOB AVB 2026, Ziff. 15.1: "Das Urheberrecht verbleibt bei der Beauftragten". Ziff. 15.2 grants free use "zur Vollendung des Projektes für ihre Bedürfnisse".\[521\] Whether ML training is covered is a question of interpretation, and older plans follow whatever contract applied.
- **Competition entries stay the participants' copyright** (SIA 142 Art. 26.1; §4.4).
- **Unfair competition law also names plans.** UWG Art. 5 makes it unfair to exploit "ein ihm anvertrautes Arbeitsergebnis wie Offerten, Berechnungen oder Pläne" without authorisation, or to take over another's work result "ohne angemessenen eigenen Aufwand durch technische Reproduktionsverfahren". This is relevant to tender documents, permit files and portals even where copyright does not apply.\[524\]

**Art. 24d URG: text and data mining for scientific research** (in force since 1 April 2020).
- **Text:** "Zum Zweck der wissenschaftlichen Forschung ist es zulässig, ein Werk zu vervielfältigen, wenn die Vervielfältigung durch die Anwendung eines technischen Verfahrens bedingt ist und zu den zu vervielfältigenden Werken ein rechtmässiger Zugang besteht." Copies may be kept for archiving and backup after the research (para. 2). Computer programs are excluded (para. 3).\[400\]
- **Dispatch (BBl 2018 591):**
  - The exception "erfasst bewusst auch die kommerzielle Forschung".
  - It covers basic and applied research, but "wissenschaftliche Forschung [muss] Hauptzweck der Arbeiten bleiben".
  - No remuneration is due.
  - It covers reproduction only, not making available.
  - No use of the copies "über das «Text- und Data-Mining» hinaus" is allowed.
  - Operational documentation stays under Art. 19 para. 1 lit. c.\[401\]
- **No opt-out and an open question on contracts.** Swiss law has no opt-out. Whether contracts can override Art. 24d is unresolved: the URG makes only the software backup copy expressly non-waivable (Art. 24 para. 3). One practitioner view holds contractual training bans "dürften regelmässig unwirksam sein".\[525\]
- **AI training is undecided.** IPI: the provision "wurde nicht mit Blick auf das KI-Training eingeführt. Ob sie auf solche Fälle Anwendung finden kann, müssen die Gerichte entscheiden". The IPI plans a consultation draft on AI and copyright "bis Ende 2026".\[402\] Motion 24.4596 (Gössi), asking for "umfassend Schutz" of works used by AI providers, was transferred to the Federal Council on 11 December 2025. The National Council had struck out the original opt-in requirement and the clause that would have denied AI providers the exceptions of Art. 19 para. 3, 24a, 24d and 28.\[526\]
- **Lawful access is a condition.** Bypassing DRM or access controls is excluded (Art. 39a). Copying whole commercially available books for internal use is not allowed (Art. 19 para. 3 lit. a); internal copies under Art. 19 para. 1 lit. c carry remuneration (Art. 20).\[400\]

**EU rules where sources or acts are in the EU.** Copyright follows the law of the country for which protection is claimed (IPRG Art. 110).\[527\]
- DSM Directive Art. 3 allows TDM for scientific research by research organisations and cultural heritage institutions, and contracts cannot override it (Art. 7(1)).
- Art. 4 allows TDM by anyone unless the rights holder has reserved it "in an appropriate manner, such as machine-readable means" (Art. 4(3)).\[528\]
- Germany implements these as §60d and §44b UrhG; a reservation for online works "ist nur dann wirksam, wenn er in maschinenlesbarer Form erfolgt" (§44b(3)).\[529\] A publisher's §44b reservation (De Gruyter, ImmoScout24.de) therefore matters for any copying done in the EU or under German-law licences.
- The AI Act's duty to respect Art. 4(3) reservations (Art. 53(1)(c)) binds providers of general-purpose AI models, not a task-specific extractor.\[530\]
- **Case law is moving:**
  - OLG Hamburg (10 Dec 2025, LAION) held dataset creation to be TDM under §44b and §60d. The BGH decision (I ZR 281/25) is announced for 17 December 2026.\[531\]
  - LG München I (GEMA v. OpenAI, 11 Nov 2025) found memorised lyrics reproduced in model parameters and held that this is not TDM.\[532\]
  - A Dutch court found a robots.txt that blocked only some AI bots not to be a sufficient reservation (ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2024:6563).\[533\]

**Internal evaluation versus production training.**
- **Evaluation:** comparing extraction output with reference plans is automated analysis to generate information. It is likely to fit Art. 24d and the DSM definition of TDM (Art. 2(2)), provided the copies stay internal, are made for that study and lawful access exists.
- **Training production models:** this moves from research towards operational use, which the dispatch places outside Art. 24d. Whether trained weights reproduce training works is contested: LG München I found reproduction where content was memorised; the English High Court found that a model which stores no works is not an infringing copy (Getty v. Stability AI, 2025); the Court of Justice of the EU has the question pending (C-250/25).\[532\]\[534\]\[535\]
- **Practical rule** (as in [datasets-open.md](datasets-open.md#1-scope-and-method)): use rights-reserved, NC and permission-only material for evaluation at most. Train production weights only on public-domain or permissively licensed material, BBL's own plans with confirmed contractual rights, or material licensed for training in writing.

## 6. Gaps

1. **No external source of office or administrative plans with room stamps** (number, usage, area) under a licence that allows training. Only peer owners' CAFM DWGs carry these labels.
2. **No vendor sells non-residential annotated plans.** All commercial corpora found are residential (CubiCasa, Floorplanner, Manycore, Realsee, Matterport), and annotation is custom work.
3. **Swiss historical plans are digitised but locked.** e-periodica, KdS-online and Staatsarchiv Zürich hold tens of thousands of plans, but none allows systematic reuse without an agreement. The largest analogue holdings (EAD, BAR, gta, building-permit archives) are barely digitised.
4. **Licence prices are unpublished.** No price was found for BDT institutional or TDM licences, the e-periodica rights holders, DETAIL, GSK or archive bulk agreements.
5. **No copyright status per plan.** Author death dates are not recorded in catalogues, so the 70-year rule cannot be applied in bulk.
6. **No open Swiss indoor geometry.** OpenStreetMap has 1,947 rooms nationwide; opendata.swiss has building footprints only.
